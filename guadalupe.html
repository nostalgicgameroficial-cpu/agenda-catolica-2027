PATRONESS OF THE AMERICAS

WHY OUR LADY OF GUADALUPE

In December 1531, Mary appeared to St. Juan Diego on Tepeyac Hill, near present-day Mexico City. Her image on his tilma has drawn pilgrims for nearly five centuries. She is honored as Patroness of the Americas, and her feast is celebrated on December 12.

This edition places the year under her mantle. Open it each morning, bring her your intentions, and let her lead you to her Son.

"Am I not here, who am your Mother? Are you not under my shadow and protection?" — Words of Our Lady to St. Juan Diego
